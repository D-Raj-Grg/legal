## Getting started

ExtraHelper is a staff app. Your restaurant's owner or manager creates your account and sets your
role, then you sign in with the email address they used. If you don't have an account yet, ask them
first — you cannot sign yourself up.

---

## Common questions

### I can't sign in

Check that you are using the email address your manager registered, and that you have a network
connection for the first sign-in. After the first successful sign-in the app works offline.

### The app says configuration is missing

The build was packaged without its server settings. Contact us — this is not something you can fix
from the device.

### My printer isn't found

- **Bluetooth printers** — make sure the printer is powered on, in range, and paired in your phone's
  Bluetooth settings first. Then grant ExtraHelper the Bluetooth permission when asked.
- **WiFi printers** — the phone and the printer must be on the same network. Grant the local network
  permission when prompted.

If you declined a permission earlier, re-enable it in your phone's Settings under ExtraHelper.

### Orders aren't syncing

ExtraHelper saves orders on the device and syncs them when the connection returns. Check your WiFi or
mobile data. Orders taken offline are not lost — they upload automatically once you're back online.

### The camera won't scan a label

Grant the camera permission in Settings under ExtraHelper. If the barcode is damaged or missing, you
can always search for the item by name instead.

### I need my account removed

Ask your restaurant's owner or manager to remove you, or email us and we will action it within 30
days. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) for detail on what is deleted and what is retained as a
business record.
