# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 14 August 2026
**Last updated:** 14 August 2026
**App:** ExtraHelper (iOS and Android)
**Developer:** D. Raj Gurung
**Contact:** [divy5831@gmail.com](mailto:divy5831@gmail.com)

This Privacy Policy describes how ExtraHelper ("the app," "we," "our," or "us") handles information
when you use the application.

ExtraHelper is a workplace tool. It is used by restaurant staff to take orders, send tickets to the
kitchen, print bills, and count stock. It is not a consumer app, and it is not intended for use by
the general public or by children.

---

## 1. Who controls your data

ExtraHelper accounts are created and managed by the **restaurant that employs you**, not by us. That
restaurant decides who has an account, what role each person holds, and what happens to the records
its staff create.

In data protection terms, the restaurant is the **controller** of the information in its workspace.
We operate the software on the restaurant's behalf as a **processor**. If you are a staff member and
you want your personal information corrected or removed, speak to the restaurant's owner or manager
first — they control the account. You may also contact us at the address above and we will assist.

---

## 2. Information we handle

### 2.1 Account information

- **Email address** — used to sign you in and to identify you to your team.
- **Password** — never stored in readable form. Authentication is handled by Supabase, and passwords
  are stored only as salted hashes.
- **Display name and role** — for example Owner, Manager, or Staff, set by your restaurant.
- **User ID** — a random identifier that links your actions to your account.

### 2.2 Operational records you create

While doing your job in the app, you create business records such as:

- Orders, order items, custom items, and their prices
- Table and floor status
- Kitchen tickets and their routing between stations
- Bills, payments, and receipt history
- Store room counts and inventory adjustments
- Manager log entries and shift notes

These records belong to the restaurant. They describe restaurant operations, not your private life.
Where a record shows who performed an action, that is deliberate — it is how a restaurant maintains
accountability over cash and stock.

### 2.3 Guest information

ExtraHelper is designed so that restaurant guests stay anonymous. We do not ask you to record a
guest's name, phone number, email, or payment card details, and the app provides no field for them.
Payment QR codes printed on a bill are generated from the restaurant's own payment details, not the
guest's.

If a member of staff types personal information about a guest into a free-text field such as an
order note, that text is stored like any other order data. Please don't do this.

---

## 3. Device permissions

The app asks for the following permissions. Each is used for one narrow purpose, and you can decline
any of them and still use the rest of the app.

- **Camera** — to scan barcodes and QR codes on item labels during a store room count. Frames are
  processed on the device to read the code. Images are never saved, never uploaded, and never leave
  your phone. You can always search for an item by name instead.
- **Bluetooth** — to discover and connect to a nearby thermal printer so the app can print bills and
  kitchen tickets. Used only for printing. We do not use Bluetooth for location, presence detection,
  proximity marketing, or analytics.
- **Local network** — to send print jobs to a thermal printer on the same WiFi network. Used only for
  printing. The app does not scan the network for any other purpose.

---

## 4. Where your data is stored

Account and operational data are stored in a **Supabase** project operated by us on the restaurant's
behalf. Supabase provides the database and authentication infrastructure and hosts it on cloud
servers. Access is enforced by row-level security so that a restaurant's staff can reach only that
restaurant's records.

The app also keeps a **local copy on your device** so it keeps working when the internet drops. This
cache lives in the app's private storage, is not readable by other apps, and syncs back to the server
once you are online again. Signing out or deleting the app removes it.

Data transmitted between the app and the server is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

---

## 5. What we do not do

- We do **not** include advertising SDKs. The app shows no ads.
- We do **not** include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites, and we do not use the iOS Advertising
  Identifier.
- We do **not** sell, rent, or lease any information to anyone, ever.
- We do **not** collect your location. The app has no location permission.
- We do **not** access your contacts, photo library, microphone, calendar, or health data.

---

## 6. Sharing

We share information only in these situations:

- **With your restaurant.** Your colleagues and managers see the records you create, according to the
  role your restaurant assigned you.
- **With infrastructure providers.** Supabase stores the data so the service can run. It acts on our
  instructions and does not use the data for its own purposes.
- **When legally required.** If we receive a valid legal order, we may have to disclose information.
  We will tell the affected restaurant unless the law forbids it.

---

## 7. Retention and deletion

Operational records are kept for as long as the restaurant maintains its account, because businesses
need historical sales and stock records.

To delete your account, ask your restaurant's owner or manager to remove you, or email us at
[divy5831@gmail.com](mailto:divy5831@gmail.com) and we will action the request within 30 days. When
an entire restaurant closes its account, its data is deleted within 90 days of the request.

Note that deleting a staff account does not erase the restaurant's sales history. Orders remain as
business records; the personal account attached to them is removed.

---

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your
personal information, and to object to certain processing. Email us and we will help. Because your
restaurant controls the workspace, we may need to route the request through them.

---

## 9. Children

ExtraHelper is a workplace tool for restaurant staff. It is not directed at children, and we do not
knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has created an account,
contact us and we will remove it.
