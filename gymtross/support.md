## Getting started

Gym Tross has two sides in one app.

**Staff** — your gym's owner or branch manager creates your account and sets your role, then you sign
in with the email address they used. If you don't have an account yet, ask them first — you cannot
sign yourself up.

**Members** — your gym registers you and sends an invite email. Open the link on your phone, set a
password, and you're in. You cannot register yourself.

---

## Common questions

### I can't sign in

Check that you are using the email address your gym registered, and that you have a network
connection. If you have never set a password, use the invite email your gym sent rather than the
sign-in form.

### The invite link doesn't open the app

Open the link on the phone that has Gym Tross installed, and tap it from the email app rather than
pasting it into a browser. If it still opens a web page instead of the app, tell your gym — the link
may have expired. They can resend it.

### I forgot my password

Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen. The reset email goes to the address your gym
registered for you. If you no longer have access to that address, ask your gym to change it on your
record first.

### The app says configuration is missing

The build was packaged without its server settings. Contact us — this is not something you can fix
from the device.

### The QR scanner won't read a member's code

Grant the camera permission in Settings under Gym Tross. Make sure the code is in focus and the
screen showing it is bright enough. If it still won't read, look the member up by name or phone
instead — check-in works the same way.

### I can't take a member's photo

Grant the camera permission, or the photo library permission if you are picking an existing image.
If you declined either earlier, re-enable it in your phone's Settings under Gym Tross.

### I can't see any members, or the plan list is empty

You are probably scoped to a branch with no data, or to no branch at all. Use the branch switcher at
the top of the staff screens to pick the right branch. If the branch is right and the list is still
empty, ask your manager to check that your account is assigned to that branch and that the branch has
plans set up.

### A payment I recorded shows the wrong amount

Payments are records, not edits — correct one with a refund or an adjustment rather than retyping it,
so the collection sheet still balances. If you cannot do that from your role, ask your manager.

### Something I can do on the web console isn't in the app

The app covers front-desk work: registration, check-in, payments, renewals, and the visitor log.
Reporting, plan setup, staff management, and org settings live in the web console at
[app.lordofgyms.com](https://app.lordofgyms.com).

### I need my account removed

Ask your gym's owner or branch manager to remove you, or email us and we will action it within 30
days. See [Account Deletion](delete-account.html) for what is deleted and what is retained as a
business record.
